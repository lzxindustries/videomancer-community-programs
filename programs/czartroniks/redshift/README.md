# Redshift

Treats the picture's brightness as mass: the image bends, spins and red/blue-shifts around its own bright areas, with photon rings, debris streaks, and cores collapsing into voids or white holes.

**P12 (Collapse):** Master gravity: 0 is exactly dry, and rising adds deflection, darkens space, engages the horizon and speeds up debris. 100% is black space with ringed singularities and spaghettified light.

## Controls

| | Control | Range | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Mass | 0–100% | How bright something must be to count as mass |
| K2 | Spin | -100–100% | Twists the image around masses (centre = none) |
| K3 | Shells | 0–100% | Width of glowing rings around masses |
| K4 | Doppler | 0–100% | Blue/red colour shift and fringes |
| K5 | Debris | 0–100% | Amount of white streaks falling into masses |
| K6 | Horizon | 0–100% | How easily black cores form |
| S7 | Polarity | Bright Mass / Dark Mass | Bright or dark areas act as mass |
| S8 | Core | Void / White Hole | Cores are black voids or inverted video |
| S9 | Force | Attract / Repel | Image pulled toward or pushed from masses |
| S10 | Turbulence | Off / On | On: boiling jittery warping |
| S11 | Pulse | Off / On | On: gravity slowly pulses |
| P12 | Collapse | 0–100% | Overall gravity strength: clean to full collapse |

## Presets

Gravity Well, Ergosphere, White Hole, Total Collapse.

## Technical notes

- Program type: processing; core `yuv444_30b`.
- Input-to-output latency: 16 clocks (`processing_delay_clks`).
- Built and timed at the full 74.25 MHz HD pixel clock.
